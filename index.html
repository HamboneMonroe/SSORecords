THE SUNDAY SOAP OPERA

Sixteen Years of Heartbreak, Unsolved Draft Disasters, and Pure Fantasy Chaos

Welcome to The Sunday Soap Opera

Back in 2010, the league humble-bragged its way into existence as a modest 4-team showdown where everyone was guaranteed a playoff spot. Over the next sixteen years, that cozy circle expanded into a full 12-member circus where grown adults log onto Yahoo Fantasy every September to willingly subject themselves to sixteen weeks of unadulterated pain, irrational lineup decisions, and relentless smack talk. What started as a small quad has mutated into a multi-decade saga filled with undeniable dynasties, cosmic bad luck, and managerial trades so baffling they ought to be investigated by a joint federal task force.

The DeVante Adams Curse (White Bronco)

Legend has it that in the dawn of a rookie draft long ago, Bacon (managing White Bronco) accidentally selected WR DeVante Adams 1st overall in his rookie season. Some blamed local Brantford Wi-Fi. Some blamed an unsanctioned second gin and tonic. Bacon maintains it was a technological glitch. The fantasy gods, however, cared not for excuses. Since that fateful click, White Bronco has compiled a staggering 139 career losses—the most in league history—and has been officially barred from playoff wins for what feels like eternity. Some say bad management, some say bad internet... I guess we'll never know.

The Myth of the 2008 Detroit Lions

In 2017, Doug took over the 2008 Detriot Lions franchise and achieved true immortality by finishing 0–13–0. The team was a smoking crater. Enter Nate in 2018. Nate hijacked the keys to the zero-win franchise, rode into the season like a fantasy mercenary, dropped 1,962 points, went 10–3, swept through the playoff bracket, and won the 2018 Championship! Having accomplished the impossible, Nate promptly handed the keys back to its original owner and walked into the sunset like a true wildcard hero.

The Dynasties: Steven & Matt

While the rest of us fight over waiver-wire kickers, Steven (Steeltown Junkies) and Matt (RUN CMC) have been operating corporate empires. Steven holds the crown for most championships in league history with three titles (2015, 2020, 2025), accompanied by an astonishing 116 regular-season wins. Meanwhile, Matt entered the league in 2018 and immediately turned it into his personal piggy bank, posting a ridiculous .684 playoff win percentage with two titles (2019, 2023) and five podium finishes.

The Eternal Bridesmaid: Dan & Clenched Tuxedos

Nobody rides the emotional roller coaster quite like Dan (Hatchley Sooners). Dan holds the undisputed league record for heartbreak, finishing as the Championship Runner-Up three separate times (2016, 2017, 2019). In a desperate bid to end his curse, Dan joined forces with Nate in 2025 to create Clenched Tuxedos—only to finish 5–9 and miss the playoffs entirely.
